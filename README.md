# 🍦 Gelateria Bolognese - Organizational Chaos Game! 🍦

A hilarious browser-based game about managing a gelateria (ice cream shop) in Bologna, Italy, where organizational design meets Italian chaos!

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Manage your staff, make decisions, and survive daily challenges
3. Balance money, staff happiness, and reputation
4. Try to survive 30 days and build a thriving gelateria empire!

## 🎯 Game Objective

Survive the daily chaos of running a gelateria in Bologna! You need to:

- **Manage Money**: Keep your finances positive by making smart choices
- **Keep Staff Happy**: Unhappy staff will quit and leave you stranded
- **Maintain Reputation**: Bad reviews will sink your business
- **Make it to Day 30**: Survive 30 days with over €200 to win!

## 🎲 Game Mechanics

### Stats to Track
- 💰 **Money**: Your financial health (don't go broke!)
- 😊 **Staff Happiness**: Keep it above 0% or face a rebellion
- ⭐ **Reputation**: Maintain your image in Bologna
- 📅 **Day Counter**: Survive 30 days to win

### Staff Management
- **Hire New Staff**: Costs €30 per hire
- **Change Roles**: Reassign staff to different positions
- **Fire Staff**: Decrease morale but save on salaries
- **Daily Salaries**: Automatically deducted each day

### Available Roles
- **Scooper** (€5/day): Serves gelato to customers
- **Cashier** (€5/day): Handles money and transactions
- **Gelato Maker** (€8/day): Creates new gelato flavors
- **Manager** (€10/day): Manages the chaos
- **Social Media Influencer** (€7/day): Takes photos for Instagram

## 😄 Funny Scenarios

Each day brings new organizational challenges:

- Bus loads of tourists overwhelming your shop
- Staff conflicts about cleaning duties
- Food bloggers demanding "flat organizational structures"
- Rival gelaterias poaching your talent
- City inspectors questioning your job descriptions
- Family drama when you hire relatives
- And many more hilarious situations!

## 🏆 Winning Conditions

- Survive 30 days
- Have more than €200 in the bank
- Keep staff happiness and reputation above 0%

## 💀 Losing Conditions

- **Bankruptcy**: Money drops below €0
- **Staff Rebellion**: Happiness drops to 0%
- **Reputation Destroyed**: Reputation drops to 0%
