# ⚡ METAL MAYHEM BOLOGNA ⚡

An interactive browser-based game about managing a heavy metal band in Bologna's underground scene, where organizational psychology meets rock & roll chaos!

## 🎸 About The Game

**Metal Mayhem Bologna** teaches real organizational psychology theories through the lens of managing a metal band. Navigate group dynamics, leadership challenges, motivation dilemmas, and team conflicts while trying to survive 30 weeks in Bologna's brutal underground music scene!

## 🧠 Educational Psychology Theories Covered

This game integrates authentic organizational and social psychology theories:

### Group Development
- **Tuckman's Stages**: Forming → Storming → Norming → Performing
- Watch your band evolve through these stages based on your decisions

### Leadership Theories
- **Autocratic vs. Democratic vs. Laissez-faire** leadership styles
- **Situational Leadership**: Different contexts require different approaches
- Learn when to be directive, participative, supportive, or delegative

### Motivation Theories
- **Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs**: Basic needs before self-actualization
- **Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory**: Hygiene factors vs. motivators
- Understand intrinsic vs. extrinsic motivation

### Team Dynamics
- **Social Identity Theory**: How group membership shapes commitment
- **Belbin's Team Roles**: Different roles contribute to team success
- **Social Loafing (Ringelmann Effect)**: Individual accountability matters
- **Groupthink (Janis)**: Dangers of harmony over critical thinking

### Conflict & Communication
- **Conflict Resolution Styles**: Compete, collaborate, avoid, compromise
- **Psychological Contract Theory**: Unwritten expectations and trust
- **Fundamental Attribution Error**: Situational vs. dispositional causes

### Organizational Behavior
- **Organizational Culture**: Values, beliefs, and norms
- **Psychological Safety**: Creating space for innovation and dissent

## 🎭 Scenarios Based on Real Psychology

Every decision teaches you something:

### Example: Tuckman's Storming Stage
*"Marco and Giulia are fighting about who gets center stage..."*
- **Compete**: Let them fight (authentic but destructive)
- **Collaborate**: Design staging where both shine (moves to Norming!)
- **Avoid**: Cancel the gig (damages reputation)

### Example: Maslow's Hierarchy
*"Your drummer is sleeping in his van, can barely eat..."*
- **Motivational speeches**: FAIL - can't motivate when survival needs aren't met!
- **Cover his rent collectively**: SUCCESS - basic needs enable focus
- **Help find day job**: Practical compromise

### Example: Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory
*"Your sound tech wants a raise..."*
- **Money only**: Temporary fix (hygiene factor)
- **Recognition + fair pay**: True satisfaction (hygiene + motivator!)
- **Recognition without pay**: Feels patronizing

## 🧪 Psychological Theories Reference

### Tuckman's Group Development (1965)
Teams progress through: Forming (polite, uncertain) → Storming (conflict) → Norming (cohesion) → Performing (high function)

### Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs (1943)
Physiological → Safety → Belonging → Esteem → Self-Actualization
(Lower needs must be met before higher ones motivate)

### Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory (1959)
- **Hygiene factors** (salary, conditions): Prevent dissatisfaction
- **Motivators** (recognition, growth): Create satisfaction

### Social Identity Theory - Tajfel & Turner (1979)
People derive identity from group membership; strong in-group identity increases commitment

### Belbin's Team Roles (1981)
Effective teams need diverse roles: Coordinators, Shapers, Implementers, etc.

### Groupthink - Irving Janis (1972)
Desire for harmony can override critical thinking; devil's advocates improve decisions

### Ringelmann Effect (1913)
Social loafing: Individuals exert less effort in groups when contributions aren't identifiable

### Fundamental Attribution Error (Ross, 1977)
We attribute others' failures to character while ignoring situational factors
